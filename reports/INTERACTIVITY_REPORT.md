# Interactivity report: making Scrap Bot interactive at all times

Generated by `tools/write_interactivity_report.py` from the JSON in `results/`; every table below is read from a measurement file, not typed. Audit and evidence: `docs/INTERACTIVITY_AUDIT.md`. Branch `main`.

## 1. Summary

1. The audit found none of E1-E5 built, so all five were built, each behind a default-off flag in `data/flags.json`, and tested with the existing sim, bots and screenshots.
2. With every flag off the sim reproduces the stored baseline fingerprint of 36 bot runs exactly (`tests/bots/trace_hash.gd` prints `TRACE MATCH`); the full test suite passes (25,220 checks, 0 failures).
3. With all flags on, an aiming bot is never more than about 3 s from a player-decided outcome in Level 1 (gap median 2.2 s, 100 % of runs <= 4 s; the baseline had 0 %), the chosen first part shows its effect in 0.3 s instead of 3.3 s, and win rates for the aiming bots stay at 100 %.
4. Desktop performance holds (p95 17.4 ms vs 17.3 ms, draw calls max 146 vs 133, under the 150 budget); **phone performance, human playtests and store-level checks were NOT run**.
5. Nothing is turned on by default. Section 9 recommends which flags to enable for a first A/B playtest.

## 3. What was built

All switches are in `data/flags.json`; override per run with `-- --flags='{"aim_mode":"lane"}'` or `RunSim.flag_overrides`. Logging: `engagement_metrics` (see section 4).

### E1 Steering is aiming: flags `aim_mode` (`lane` | `nearest`), `guarded_salvage`
- **Files**: `src/sim/run_sim.gd` (`_pick_target`, `_fire`, `_add_salvage_beats`, `_spawn_salvage`), `src/view/world_view.gd` (`_build_aim_feedback`, `_sync_aim`), `src/view/mesh_factory.gd` (crate), `data/enemies.json` (`crate`), `tests/bots/aim_bot.gd`.
- **Behaviour**: in lane mode the nearest enemy inside a 12 degree half-angle cone of the robot's lane is the target; with nothing in the cone, weapons still fire straight ahead (range 24 m, 30 m while gate charge is on). Legacy mode is untouched. A ring marks the locked target. Guarded salvage places two lane-specific targets side by side (lanes at x = +/-2.0, side chosen from the seeded spawn stream): an explosive barrel between two gunners (one shot clears both guards) and a loot crate (90 hp, 30 scrap + a 10-coin burst) behind a gunner. `BLAST` / `LOOT` tags float over them. Level 1 gets one block at z=215; generated levels get one after every second gate.
- **Tuned**: `aim_cone_deg` 12 (the prompt's start value, read as a half-angle; UNVERIFIED reading). Crate hp 90 (about 7 cannon hits of 14 damage) so the guarded route costs real fire.
- **Checks**: an enemy outside the cone is not hit until the player steers into its lane (test); aiming bots with 0 / 150 / 250 / 400 ms reaction still win 100 % of Level 1 runs (section 4); projectile cap 64, hostile cap 16, enemy cap hold every tick for all four delays on Level 3 with all flags on (test).

### E2 Charge the gates, instant payoff: flags `gate_charge`, `post_gate_payoff`
- **Files**: `run_sim.gd` (`_bullet_charge`, `_charge_gate`, `_charge_cone`, `charge_bonus`, `charge_cost`, `_spawn_payoff`), `world_view.gd` (`_charge_panel`, `_update_charge`), `run_view.gd` (hint, events), `data/flags.json`.
- **Behaviour**: every bullet (or flame sweep) crossing a part panel within 30 m adds charge worth `damage / build dps`, i.e. 1.0 per second of fully focused fire for any build. Each panel's thresholds are 1.0 s and 3.0 s times a per-part cost and raise the granted level by one each, up to the level cap 3. Panels show a charge bar and the level and name that holding the lane would grant (`LV 2 TWIN CANNONS`).
- **Window and speed**: forward speed 9.0 m/s (first gate at z=80 is crossed at 8.9 s). Charge range 30 m = 3.3 s of charging; the gate is already readable at 36 m (4.0 s), see `results/interactivity_first_gate_e2.png`.
- **Choice pressure**: the total charge available (3.3 s) is below what maxes both panels for every pair of parts that can share a gate (test over all part pairs), and the per-part costs (cannon 1.1, rocket 1.0, flame 1.0, wheels 0.6, tracks 0.8, armour 0.8, shield 1.0, magnet 1.3, storage 1.2) make strong parts need more charge than weak ones.
- **Payoff after gate 1**: 14 m past the gate a formation shows the chosen part: weapons get a tight cluster of 4 scrappers, movement a 14-coin swerving bonus route, defence two gunners, collection a 16-coin scatter.
- **Content fix (retuning, flagged)**: Level 1's first gate offered Cannon against Wheels, a weapon against no weapon (baseline: the bot that judges by playthrough picks Cannon 69 %, with a 25 % score gap). With `post_gate_payoff` on it offers **Cannon vs Storage Pack** (`level1_first_gate`). Wheels still get their bonus route wherever they appear (tested; generated levels).
- **Checks**: time to first payoff and pick shares below.

### E3 Timing: flags `moving_gates`, `focus_fire`
- **Files**: `run_sim.gd` (`gate_offset`, `panel_center`, `_panel_lane`, `focus_multiplier`), `world_view.gd` (narrow sliding panels, ground rail), `tests/bots/moving_fairness.gd`, `tests/bots/focus_check.gd`.
- **Moving gates**: from the third gate on, every other two-panel pair (Level 1: z=290 and z=520) slides +/-0.9 m with a 5 s period (peak 1.13 m/s, test < 1.5 m/s), panels 2.4 m wide, 2.8 m apart. The slide is a pure function of sim time (deterministic). An orange ground rail shows the whole travel. The panel nearest the robot wins, so passing between panels never loses the pick.
- **focus_fire** (off, A/B hypothesis only): holding a lane 0.4 s without steering ramps fire rate up to +30 % over 0.6 s; any steering resets it.
- **Checks**: fairness and focus tables below.

### E4 Rhythm and density: flag `dense_blocks`
- **Files**: `data/blocks.json`, `run_sim.gd` (`_add_rhythm_beats`, `_spawn_wave`, `_spawn_coin_arc`, `_scaled`), `run_view.gd` (`_govern_density`).
- **Blocks**: Level 1 adds three survival waves (z=315, 545, 695) and three steering coin arcs (z=40, 425, 640) around the existing gates, packs, hazards, Hauler (z=450, the convoy block) and salvage (z=215). Without them the only unfilled stretch before the boss was the 5 s run-in after the final choice. Generated levels get a coin arc before each gate, a wave after every gate (or every second one with salvage on) and a pre-boss wave. After the first gate, major gate choices stay 11-13 s apart (8.9 s to the first).
- **Density**: pack and wave sizes ramp up to +50 % at the boss; the enemy cap is 14 in this mode (24 normally) because 24 live enemies pushed draw calls to 154; a governor lowers the density limit (floor 0.6) when p95 frame time exceeds 18.5 ms or the highest draw-call count of the last 2 s exceeds 135, and raises it back when healthy. All instanced/pooled, no physics bodies.
- **Checks**: gap table below; performance table below.

### E5 One weapon evolution: flag `weapon_evolution`
- **Choice**: Cluster Rockets, the cheapest option because it only needs a hook where a rocket already hits something.
- **Requirement**: the existing Burst Volley combo (cannon + rocket) and rocket level 2. On impact a rocket splits into 3 orange bomblets (50 % damage, 1.2 m blast) that seek nearby enemies or fan out ahead; at most 12 live at once; bomblets never split again.
- **Showing what is missing**: the existing hint line extends `ROCKET + ? -> BURST VOLLEY` with `CLUSTER ROCKETS`, shows `ROCKET LV2 NEEDED -> CLUSTER ROCKETS` once the combo is active, and gate panels get an `EVOLVES!` tag when the pick would complete it.
- **Checks**: triggers only with the requirement met, flag off is inert, caps hold (tests); readable in a screenshot (`results/interactivity_e5_evolution.png`).

### Gate pick share (paired seeds, forced option at each Level 1 gate pair)

Method (`tests/bots/pair_pick.gd`, 24 seeds, bot with the existing 28 % missed-telegraph model so that defence parts matter): each seed is played twice, forcing option A then B; `score = 1000*won + 100*final HP fraction + 0.25*scrap collected - 2*boss seconds` (my weights, UNVERIFIED as a model of player value). "Paired" is how often A scores higher; because the Level 1 layout is deterministic the gaps are small but consistent, so that column goes to 0 % or 100 % at tiny differences and is shown only for honesty. "Noise-aware" models a chooser who judges each option from one playthrough and cannot perceive differences below 2 % of the score. Target 40-60 %.

## 7. Playtest checklist

1. Build A with all flags off (today's game) and build B with `aim_mode=lane, guarded_salvage, gate_charge, post_gate_payoff, moving_gates, dense_blocks, weapon_evolution` on; turn `engagement_metrics` on in both. Keep `focus_fire` off in B, and add a third build B+ with it on if you want that A/B.
2. Five new players per build, Level 1 only, nothing explained beyond "play". Record `user://telemetry.jsonl` (`run_metrics`) and the screen.
3. After the first run ask exactly two questions: **"What did your choices change?"** and **"When were you bored?"**. Note the timestamps they point at and compare with `max_engagement_gap`.
4. Watch for: players who never move off the centre lane; players who ignore the gate bars; confusion about `BLAST` / `LOOT`; the moment they notice Cluster Rockets; whether the first gate choice (Cannon vs Storage Pack) feels fair.
5. On a phone: run `tools/phone_perf_session.sh` for build B, check p95 <= 16.7 ms, draw calls <= 150, PSS <= 400 MB, thermal state after three runs.

## 8. UNVERIFIED items and assumptions

- Phone performance, memory (PSS), thermals, touch feel, and every human-perception claim: not measured.
- The 12 degree cone is read as a half-angle (24 degrees wide). A 12 degree full angle would be harder; the value is in `data/flags.json`.
- The engagement metric is my own definition (`docs/INTERACTIVITY_AUDIT.md` section 7); in particular a coin pickup counts when the target position moved at least 0.3 m in the last second, and a shot counts when the target is within 1.2 m of the robot's lane. Different thresholds shift the absolute gaps.
- The pick-share score weights (section 4) are mine. The first-gate swap to Storage Pack is a content decision I made to meet the 40-60 % target; the alternative is to keep Wheels and strengthen them.
- The Level 1 data still names `elite_bruiser` for the mid-level beat; code ignores it.
- With the flags on, the weaker bots win noticeably more on levels 3-5 (section 4); the default difficulty was not retuned.
- Bots are scripted; they do not model human reaction to the new UI, attention, or fun.
- Moving gates are easy at every delay tested; whether that is too easy is a playtest question.
- Draw-call peak: 154 with an enemy cap of 24, 146 with 14. The governor uses sampled frame times, which on desktop are vsync-quantised.
- Reaction delay in the existing bots only affects boss telegraphs, so "delayed-reaction bots" for E1 means the new `aim` bot (stale enemy and panel positions).
- E2 and E3, and E4 and E5, were committed together because their sim hooks touch the same functions; the flags are independent.

2. Should the 12 degree cone be wider for touch play? (Default: 12 degree half-angle.)

Recommendation, pending the playtest and one phone run:
- **Turn on first**: `aim_mode=lane`, `gate_charge`, `post_gate_payoff`. These close the passive stretches and give the first choice an immediate result; the aiming bots stayed at 100 % wins and the first payoff moved from 3.3 s to 0.3 s.
- **Then**: `guarded_salvage` and `weapon_evolution` (cheap, readable, low risk) and `dense_blocks` once the phone shows p95 <= 16.7 ms with the governor on.
- **Optional**: `moving_gates` (fair at all tested delays, adds a timing beat; consider a faster slide).
- **Keep off**: `focus_fire` (A/B only; it lifts the stationary bot by 13 points of win rate).
- **`engagement_metrics`**: on in playtest builds only.
- Do not build the chase threat or the visible victory destination until the playtest answers "When were you bored?".
