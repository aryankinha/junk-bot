# UI and assembly review — 1 November 2026

Implemented a white/ivory home and menu system, warm sand backgrounds, dark sage text, green Play actions and orange/gold junkyard accents. The robot/world artwork is preserved. Existing progression, login rewards, upgrades, collection, settings and daily runs remain available.

## Research and design decision

Reviewed the current official [Mob Control listing](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.vincentb.MobControl), its [App Store release notes](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/mob-control/id1562817072), and the official [Count Masters listing](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=freeplay.crowdrun.com). Mob Control documents collection/Armory navigation through loadout controls; both products emphasize the immediate playable runner/combat loop. Store material is promotional and does not establish every variant of their live menus.

Our design inference: give the primary play loop visual priority and group collection management behind a contextual Garage. The white/sand palette is this project's adaptation requested by the owner, not a claim that either competitor uses an identical current layout.

Home now has four controls: Play, Garage, Daily Run, and a compact Settings control. The old home had six competing controls. Upgrades, Parts and Collection share the Garage navigation. Counters are reduced on Home; streak and level details remain within the Garage. Large outlined white-on-purple labels and purple panels were replaced with readable dark text, quiet borders and light cards.

## Bugs fixed

- Roof/side/back attachment meshes could intersect the animated head or each other. Slot positions now reserve an expanded head/antenna animation envelope and separate attachment bounds with a clearance margin. Mounting struts connect the separated components to the chassis.
- Larger wheel upgrades extended below the road, and original wheels remained visible beneath movement parts. Movement geometry is now placed above the road and original wheels are hidden when a movement upgrade is present.
- Taking Wheels after Tracks allowed both mutually overlapping movement assemblies. Movement replacements now work in both directions and retain the existing level carry-over behavior.
- Changing meta tier without changing part level left old trim on an existing robot. Tier changes now invalidate attachment meshes. The Garage preview uses current saved tiers immediately after purchase.
- Attachment and growth tweens could outlive replaced meshes or compete with subsequent stage changes. Attachment tweens are bound to their nodes and previous growth tweens are stopped before rebuilding.
- Five unused icon nodes leaked each time Parts/Armoury opened. Skipped icons are now explicitly freed. Final UI and integration runs exit cleanly.
- Android Back could reopen an old paused/finished run from a menu. Back navigation now respects the current page; purchase and repair choices are not dismissed into invalid run states.
- Inventory chips queued for deletion could temporarily participate in layout twice. They are removed from their container before deletion.
- Fixed boss/notification positions could collide with wrapped inventory rows. Boss bars, toasts, pickup chains and combo labels now follow the actual laid-out HUD height.
- Oversized previews could crop attachments/platforms. Preview cameras fit the full robot bounds and viewport aspect, including full builds and tier trim.
- Long settings/result pages could push actions outside shorter screens, especially with optional ad actions. Those pages now scroll; Garage lists retain their own scrolling.
- All menu and HUD safe-area calculations use logical viewport height rather than a hardcoded 1920-pixel conversion.

- 1,419 UI/geometry checks: zero failures. `results/ui_review_20261101_pass.txt` and `results/ui_review_20261101.json`.
- Tested head/attachment clearances at levels 1–3 across pickup orders, road clearance, movement-wheel visibility, tier refresh and Back behavior.
- Rendered Home, Upgrades, Parts, Collection, Settings, Pause, Results and the fully loaded boss HUD at 360×640, 432×768 and 600×960. Buttons fit the non-scrolling home/pause pages; the final action on scrolling pages is reachable.
- Screenshots: `results/ui_20261101_*`. The Home screenshots deliberately use a fully assembled robot to exercise large-build framing.

Assembly checks cover visual mesh envelopes, not a replacement of the game's simulation hitboxes. Human touch feel, real device cutouts and native ad presentation still need a physical phone check. No store publishing was performed.
